# NNNN. Title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who actually decided; "open" if it is still open

## Context

What forces are in play. Which of the project's constraints
this touches. What a reader who has never seen this repo needs to know before
the decision makes sense.

## Decision

What we are doing. Present tense, specific enough to check code against.

## Alternatives rejected

One subsection per alternative, each ending in why it lost. This section is the
point of the document. If it is empty, there was no decision to record.

### Alternative A

Why not.

## Consequences

What becomes easy, what becomes hard, and what we have to live with. Include the
cost of reversing this decision later.

## Revisit when

The concrete signal that should make someone reopen this. "Never" is a valid
answer; "when we have time" is not.
